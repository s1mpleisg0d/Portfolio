About

I am ZeelKumar Narola, a software engineering who began with an information technology degree in India, earned a Master of Science in Computer Science at Wright State University, and has spent close to three years building production software for teams across the United States and abroad.

Most of my work lives on the Microsoft .NET platform, in C#, SQL Server, and modern JavaScript frameworks. I have delivered web, enterprise, and distributed applications across the full lifecycle, from analysis and design through testing and deployment, including single page front ends in Angular and React and scalable Web APIs backed by well designed databases.

What pulls me now is applying machine learning with the same engineering discipline. Through AIML-500 I have been pairing model judgment with the habits that keep software dependable, because the work I care about is building tools a team can trust rather than demonstrations that look good once and break in the real world.
