About

I am a software application developer with close to three years of experience across the full software lifecycle on the Microsoft .NET platform. I work in C#, SQL Server, and modern JavaScript frameworks, and most of my recent work has centered on teaching software to generate itself.

At the State of Ohio I built AI assisted generation of database scripts, API endpoints, and interface components directly from requirement specifications. That work removed repetitive hand coding across the stack and cut form delivery from about three days to one day or less. I also built Playwright end to end automation that replaced manual regression cycles on a project once estimated at more than two years.

I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science from Wright State University, and I am now studying machine learning fundamentals at Indiana Wesleyan University. I bring the same bias toward working, honest solutions into everything I build with AI.
